ALLcodeOCEAN Glossary

Key terms of the concept in alphabetical order. Terms that are standard in English in the profession are kept as is.

Agent
An autonomous software executor solving a narrow task (fragment assessment, conflict finding, assembly). In ALLcodeOCEAN, assembly is performed by a swarm of narrow agents, not one universal AI.
Amdahl's Law
Parallelism is limited by the sequential share of coordination. E8: at p=0.99 the speedup ceiling on 1024 cores is 91.2× (limit 100×).
Anchors (Anchor References)
References to fragments inside an assembly (ref, multi_ref, transform). They let an agent point to a ready piece instead of regenerating it.
Archipelago
A metaphor for today's software landscape: scattered islands (repositories, packages, IDEs, CI/CD) loosely connected by bridges. ALLcodeOCEAN proposes an Ocean — continuous water instead of an archipelago.
Assembly
A subset of fragments S ⊆ ℱ with a composition operation ∘ that glues them into a program p. Goal: argmin Φ(S) = α·Err + β·cost + γ·incomp.
Bridge
Selective exchange between a lagoon and the open Ocean under the lagoon owner's rules: some things are imported, some never leave the perimeter.
Cambrian Explosion
A metaphor: as in the ocean of life, a sudden diversity of forms. In the Ocean of code — once fragments begin to combine freely, an explosive number of new programs is born.
CEGIS (Synthesis from Specification)
Counterexample-Guided Inductive Synthesis (SyGuS, Alur et al. 2013). A "synthesizer–verifier" loop: soundness is proven; termination with a finite grammar is proven; completeness in the general case is unattainable.
Client
A program that connects to the Ocean protocol. Many clients are welcome; there is no vendor lock-in and no API keys at the protocol level.
Compromise
An attack on a fragment. In the Ocean there is no monoculture — compromising one fragment does not bring down the system; word of the threat spreads along the currents.
Coordination
The sequential part of assembly that sets the parallelism ceiling under Amdahl's law. The top level of swarm control.
Currents (Flows)
The movement of fragments between participants. A P2P layer on Kademlia (O(log n) lookup) + gossip push-pull (O(log n) update propagation).
Defensive Prior Art (Priority)
Publishing the concept (2026-10-26) creates prior art: no one can patent what is disclosed. CC0 1.0 Universal + Art. 1265/1267 of the Russian Civil Code (inalienable authorship).
Ecosystem Fork
Carrying a suitable open system whole into your own lagoon to adapt it to your tasks. Like diverting a stream into your own pond.
Ecosystems
A metaphor for full stacks: a set of islands (packages/applications) living in symbiosis. A large form of life in the Ocean.
Epistasis
Interaction between fragments. λ ≤ 0.3 → correctness 1.0; λ = 0.7 → 0.325 (E3). The "clean interfaces" requirement is the architectural answer.
Evolution / Evolutionary Selection
Assembly variants compete; the best combinations are kept and developed — following the principles of evolutionary biology and genetic algorithms. Elitism + restarts mitigate the lack of a convergence guarantee.
Granularity / No Rigid Gradation
Plankton (micro-fragments) → schools of fish (functions/modules) → islands (packages/applications) → ecosystems (stacks). There are no sharp boundaries — you take exactly what you need: drops, a school, an island.
Knowledge Currents
A solution found on one shore reaches another. Practices and patterns spread epidemically (gossip).
Kolmogorov Complexity K(x)
The minimal length of a program that produces x. Uncomputable. "Zero excess" is a heuristic via MDL, not a mathematical optimum.
Lagoon
A closed perimeter of an organization or state: its own fragments, its own history, zero leaks. Self-sufficient, but poorer without inflow from outside. Selective exchange (a bridge) and an ecosystem fork are possible.
Legal Standing
CC0 1.0 Universal; inalienable authorship (Art. 1265/1267 of the Russian Civil Code); defensive prior art; the author is not an operator, beneficiary or founder; triple dating (DOI/OTS/notary) — in preparation.
Libraries/Packages
The Ocean's "large inhabitants" — whales and coral reefs. Mature forms honed by evolution; used whole, copied, tamed. But the foundation of life is the plankton of micro-fragments.
Lookup (Kademlia)
Finding a fragment in a network of 10⁹ nodes: on average 5.42 ± 0.83 hops (E5), maximum 8. Upper bound ceil log₂ n = 30.
Mathematical Apparatus / Navigation
The formal model (ℱ, assembly, objective function Φ), theorems (Holland's schema theorem, the Price equation, GDC sizing, NFL, Amdahl, Rice), experiments E1–E10 with seed=42.
MDL / Minimum Description Length
Rissanen's principle (1978): L(D) = min_H [L(H) + L(D|H)] — a formalization of Occam's razor. In ALLcodeOCEAN: a penalty on assembly size in the objective function; E10: an assembly 2.5× shorter at correctness 1.0.
Micro-fragment
The basic unit of code — a small fragment (function, algorithm) with an interface, pre-/postconditions, a hash, reputation, semantic tags and a measured performance profile. Not a large package.
Objective Function Φ
Φ(S) = α·Err(S, Spec) + β·cost(p(S)) + γ·incomp(S). α ≫ β, γ. cost = the full resource footprint: size, time, memory, traffic, energy.
Ocean
A single environment in which all the planet's software lives: code, history, practices. Exchange, work, storage, transfer — neighboring areas of one sea. Not an archipelago.
Plankton
A metaphor for micro-fragments: the smallest, most numerous, the foundation of the Ocean's whole food chain. From a billion drops — one water.
Queue-of-Rights (Smart Contract)
An immutable history of rights to a fragment in a CRDT (ORSet for multi-authorship). Operational transformation on conflicts. Queue-of-rights = law in code.
Reputation
A history of passed executable tests + a resource profile. A fragment's reputation accumulates on the citation graph; an author's reputation comes through the quality of their fragments.
Schools of Fish
A metaphor for functions/modules: groups of fragments working in concert. More plankton, fewer islands.
Stake
An economic stake on a fragment's correctness at publication. Protection against Sybil attacks and fragment garbage. Burned when maliciousness is detected.
Strains
Variants of one fragment with different optimizations/fixes. Evolutionary selection picks the best strains.
Swarm
A multitude of narrow specialized executors (neural and algorithmic). Each solves one task. High-class models are used selectively, where understanding of meaning is needed.
Sybil Resistance
Douceur 2002: impossible without external identification. In ALLcodeOCEAN: an economic stake (stake) at the entrance to the Ocean + reputation on the fragment citation graph, not on nodes.
Throughput
The Ocean's throughput is limited by Amdahl's law: sequential coordination/verification sets the parallelism ceiling.
Transparency
All fragments, their history, reputation and tests are open and verifiable. No black boxes.
Trust Without Blockchain
Reputation through executable tests + lightweight consensus algorithms (PBFT, Raft). Not an energy-hungry PoW blockchain as a mandatory base: trust through tests, stake and reputation on the graph.
Two-Date Chain
Publication on 2026-10-26 on GitHub; the commit history is verifiable proof of the date.
Version History / Sedimentary Layers
The history of changes in the Ocean is not lost — it settles on the bottom like geological layers. Any version of any fragment is available for audit and reuse.
Working Materials (Draft)
The concept's working materials, published on 2026-10-26.
Zero Excess
A principle: do not drag along what is not used. The basic unit of reuse is a fragment of exactly the needed size. Efficiency tests are equal to correctness tests.
